# GuardianAI Product Manifesto

## Core Mission
To provide unblinking protection for the vulnerable and undeniable truth for the innocent.
We build "Privacy-Correct" security: Total visibility where needed, absolute dignity where demanded.

## Critical Engineering Rules (The "No Compromise" List)
1. **Zone-Based Privacy (The Hardware Lock):**
   - Public/Entry Zones: Facial Recognition allowed.
   - Private Zones (Bath/Bed): Facial Recognition HARD BLOCKED. Skeletal/LiDAR only.
   - Bathroom Protocol: RGB Camera stream must be terminated at the HAL level.

2. **The Integrity Ledger (Infidelity/Tamper Mode):**
   - The Log is the Product.
   - Any sensor dropout, WiFi disconnect, or manual "Camera Off" event must be logged to the immutable ledger.
   - Secondary users cannot delete logs.

3. **Active Defense:**
   - Detect "Pre-Event Telemetry" (Intent) before the crime.
   - Example: Staggering gait + Distress audio = Medical Emergency.

4. **Interoperability:**
   - We are the "Brain" for smart home devices (Matter Protocol).
   - We infer context across devices (e.g., Cat tracking from Kitchen Cam -> Garden Cam).
